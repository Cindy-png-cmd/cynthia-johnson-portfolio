About Cynthia

I am Cynthia Johnson, professionally known as Cynthia Gatugi, a Kenyan director, actress and producer with a passion for storytelling.

I believe stories have the power to connect people, challenge perspectives and create lasting emotional experiences.

My creative interests span different genres, voices and experiences. I am committed to contributing to stories that resonate with audiences in Africa and around the world.

Through acting, directing and producing, I aim to bring meaningful stories to life and contribute to the growth of the film industry.

Looking ahead

My Vision

My vision is to build a meaningful international career in film through directing, acting and producing.

I aspire to create original films, collaborate with international studios and streaming platforms, and contribute to projects that connect audiences across cultures.

I also look forward to establishing my own production company and creating opportunities for powerful and diverse stories.

I believe African stories deserve a place on the world stage.
