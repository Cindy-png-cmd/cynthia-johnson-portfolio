Get to Know Me

About Cynthia

I am Cynthia Johnson, professionally known as Cynthia Gatugi, a Kenyan director, actress and producer with a passion for storytelling.

I believe stories have the power to connect people, challenge perspectives and create lasting emotional experiences.

My creative interests span different genres, voices and experiences. I am committed to contributing to stories that resonate with audiences in Africa and around the world.

Through directing, acting and producing, I aim to bring meaningful stories to life and collaborate with creative talents across the international film industry.

My Vision

African Stories. Global Audiences.

My ambition is to build a meaningful international career through directing, acting and producing.

I aspire to create original films, collaborate with major international studios and streaming platforms, and contribute to productions that reach audiences around the world.

My long-term vision is to establish my own production company and create opportunities for powerful African stories to take their place on the world stage.
