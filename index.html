About Cynthia

A Passion for Storytelling

I am Cynthia Johnson, professionally known as Cynthia Gatugi, a Kenyan director, actress and producer with a passion for storytelling.

I believe stories have the power to connect people, challenge perspectives and create lasting emotional experiences.

My creative interests span different genres, voices and experiences. I am committed to contributing to stories that resonate with audiences in Africa and around the world.

Through directing, acting and producing, I continue to explore the many ways film can move, inspire and connect people.

My Vision

Stories That Deserve to Be Seen and Heard

My ambition is to build a meaningful international career through directing, acting and producing.

I aspire to create original films, collaborate with international studios and streaming platforms, and contribute to productions that bring different cultures and perspectives together.

I also hope to establish my own production company and create opportunities for more stories and voices to reach the world.

I believe African stories deserve a place on the world stage.
